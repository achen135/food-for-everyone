How it works

From surplus to served, in three steps

Register and choose your role

Sign up as a food donor or a recipient organization. It takes a minute and costs nothing.

Set your location

Search your address and confirm the pin. Your organization appears on the shared map right away.

Connect with neighbors

Donors see nearby recipients; recipients see nearby donors. Reach out with the contact details on each listing.
